a game where you collect gears

you? - a tiny robot that can hold 1 item at a time and move around
	- wasd to move
	- e to pick up/drop
	- mouse to use UI
		- if an item is usable - click to use
		- click to buy in the shop
run? - robot needs [energy] to function, and [shop] sells it for [scrap]
	- if energy hits 0, game ends and you become scrap
scrap? - around the [map] there will be scrap laying around with a certain price
	items can be stepped on unless
	items:
	nut - 2
	sheet - 5
	pipe - 5
	gear - 10
	spring - 25
	camera - 50
	engine - 75
	cpu - 100
hold? - adjacent items can be picked up and dropped in a different location
energy? - robot can hold 60 points of energy max, and spends 1 every 10 seconds, and spends 1 every 10 [actions]
	this gives you 600 seconds (5 minutes) of idling, or approximately 600 tiles worth of travel minus time spent moving
action? - each pick-up, drop or move counts as a move
shop? - shop keeper buys scrap if dropped on a sell pad
	he also sells upgrades and fuel for a robot:
		20 - fuel cell - fully refreshes energy if picked up and used
		40 - energy upgrade - adds 10 to energy limit ( price goes up each time it was bought for 20 points)
		60 - stack upgrade - increases amount of items you can hold, then the item pile on your head will act as a FIFO stack
		100 - scrap collector - will automaticaly look for scrap, bringing it to the place it was dropped in. requires fuel cells to function
		200 - scrap drill - if placed near a [scrap pile] and fed fuel cells, will look for [scrap piles] to recycle them into random scrap. will not waste fuel if no piles found near
		400 - assembler - will take items around it to craft a more expensive item in exchange for power cells

map? - procedurally generated map with rooms and caves that has scrap randomly spawned in them, further from the shop - potentially more expensive and more scrap in general
